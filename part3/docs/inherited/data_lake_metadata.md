## NHANES naming convention

The current complete continuous NHANES release covers **August 2021–August 2023**. Its files use the suffix **`_L`** (for example `DEMO_L.xpt`, `DIQ_L.xpt`, and `BMX_L.xpt`), while their official public-file URLs are in the `.../Public/2021/DataFiles/` path.

## Conversion notes

- XPT files were read with pandas `read_sas(..., format="xport")` and written to typed CSV.
- The BRFSS XPT is intentionally not fully materialized as CSV: the curated CSV is a reproducible reservoir sample (seed `20260722`) and retains only the eight listed variables. `_BMI5` uses two implied decimal places in the BRFSS codebook.
- The NHANES CSV is a left join to the complete 11,933-row demographics component; questionnaire and exam fields may be blank where the person was not eligible or did not complete the component.
